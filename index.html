Our story

Craftsmen who are proud of every joint

Creative Wood Products is built on a simple belief: cabinets should be beautiful, functional, and built to outlast the house they're in. We're a full-service custom shop that designs, builds, and installs — all under one roof.

We cut every joint with care, stand behind our work, and treat your home like it's our own. When you hire us, you work directly with the people building your cabinets.

  • Solid hardwood construction, never particleboard fronts
  • In-house design, build, and installation
  • Transparent, fixed-price quotes
